Xenon2 is an AI chat assistant that runs entirely on your own Windows PC. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **your conversations never leave your computer, and we don't collect anything about you.**

## What stays on your computer

- **Your pictures.** Images you create are generated on your PC and saved in the app's folder there.
- **Your chats.** Everything you type and every reply Xenon2 writes is processed by an AI model running on your own PC. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Saved chats and settings.** Xenon2 saves your conversations, settings and chosen model in the app's own folder on your PC so they're there next time you open it.
- **"Sloth" memory.** If you turn on Sloth mode, Xenon2 remembers facts you tell it (for example, your name or preferences) so it can use them in later chats. These facts are stored only on your PC. "Goldfish" mode does not keep them.

## When Xenon2 uses the internet

Xenon2 does not need the internet. All of its AI models come inside the app when you install it from the Microsoft Store, and it works fully offline.

## What we do not do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
- We do not sell, share or receive your data, because we never have it.

## Checking your computer's hardware

To recommend a model and run it quickly, Xenon2 checks your PC's memory and graphics card on your PC. This information is not sent anywhere.

## Deleting your data

Uninstall Xenon2 from **Settings → Apps** in Windows. This removes the app, its models, your pictures (except any you saved to another folder), your saved chats and Sloth memories. You can also delete individual chats inside the app.

## Children

Xenon2 is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, since it collects no information at all.
